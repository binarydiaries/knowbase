**Analytics** helps you understand how visitors use your Agents after launch.

## What you will learn

- What the Analytics dashboard shows
- How to filter analytics by Agent and date range
- Which charts to use for improvement decisions
- Why some analytics may be gated by plan

## Open Analytics

Open **Analytics** from the sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/analytics-dashboard.png" alt="Analytics dashboard placeholder" />
</Frame>

All workspaces can see overview cards. Detailed charts are available on Starter and above.

## Filter by Agent and date

Use the header filters to view all Agents or one specific Agent. Choose a date range such as last 7 days, last 30 days, last 90 days, or this month.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/analytics-filters.png" alt="Analytics filters placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Conversations over time

Use the daily chart to spot changes in volume after a launch, campaign, website update, or new Channel rollout.

  <img src="/images/placeholders/conversations-daily-chart.png" alt="Conversations over time chart placeholder" />
