The **WhatsApp Channel** lets an Agent respond through a connected WhatsApp Business account.

## What you will learn

- How WhatsApp setup is split between Integrations and the Agent page
- How to connect a WhatsApp Business account
- How to assign a phone number to an Agent
- What to check if WhatsApp is unavailable

## Check plan access

WhatsApp is available on paid plans. If your workspace is on a free plan, the WhatsApp cards show an upgrade prompt.

  <img src="/images/placeholders/whatsapp-locked.png" alt="WhatsApp locked state placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Connect WhatsApp Business

Open **Integrations** and find **WhatsApp Business**. Enter the required Meta credentials and connect the account.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/whatsapp-integration.png" alt="WhatsApp Business integration placeholder" />
</Frame>

After connection, Agenvik lists phone numbers found in the connected account.

## Assign a number to an Agent

Open the Agent, then find **WhatsApp Channel**.

1. Choose an available phone number.
2. Assign it to the Agent.
3. Confirm that the phone number appears as assigned.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/whatsapp-channel.png" alt="WhatsApp Channel assignment placeholder" />
</Frame>

A phone number can only be assigned to one Agent at a time.

## Unassign or disconnect

Use **Unassign** on the Agent page when you want to move a number to another Agent. Use **Disconnect** from **Integrations** only when you want to remove the WhatsApp Business connection for the workspace.
