## What counts as a message

One message is one question sent to your agent plus one response back. If a user sends 5 messages in a single conversation, that counts as 5 messages toward your monthly limit. Messages you send yourself while testing in the dashboard do not count.

## What happens when you reach a limit

- **Agent limit reached**: create a new agent is disabled. Archive an unused Agent to free up a slot.
- **Knowledge source limit**: adding new sources is disabled. Delete outdated sources to free space.
- **Daily training limit reached**: the Train button is disabled until the daily reset.
- **Message limit reached**: your agent stops responding to new conversations for the rest of the billing month. You'll receive an email warning at 80% of your limit so you can upgrade before hitting the cap.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately and are charged prorated for the remainder of the month. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Within 7 days of upgrading to a paid plan, email hello@agenvik.com for a full refund, no questions asked.
