## The 5 Templates (include in caption, not the image)

**01 — Initial reply to new buyer inquiry**
```
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! 🏠
I'm [Your Name] from [Agency].

Quick questions to help me find the right property for you:
• Budget range?
• Preferred location?
• Ready possession or under construction?

I'll send you matching options shortly!
```

**02 — After-hours auto-response**
```
Hi! Thanks for messaging [Agency Name].

We're currently offline but will get back to you by [time] tomorrow.

Meanwhile, tell us:
• What type of property are you looking for?
• Your budget range?

We'll have options ready when we connect! 🏡
```

**03 — Site visit confirmation**
```
Hi [Name]! Confirming your site visit:

📍 Property: [Address]
📅 Date: [Date]
⏰ Time: [Time]

I'll be at the entrance to meet you.
Any questions before then? Reply here.
```

**04 — Follow-up after no reply (day 3)**
```
Hi [Name], just checking in 👋

You'd enquired about [property type] in [location].
Are you still looking, or has your requirement changed?

Happy to share new options if your search is still on.
```

**05 — Closing nudge**
```
Hi [Name], quick check-in on [Property Name].

Is it still on your shortlist? 
A few other buyers have shown interest this week.

Let me know if you'd like to revisit or have questions. 🙏
```
