**Integrations** are configured once per workspace and can then be used by Agents.

## What you will learn

- How the Integrations page is organized
- How WhatsApp Business is connected
- Which integrations are coming soon
- How workspace integrations relate to Agent Channels

## Open Integrations

Open **Integrations** from the sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/integrations-page.png" alt="Integrations page placeholder" />
</Frame>

## WhatsApp Business

Use **WhatsApp Business** to connect a Meta WhatsApp Business Account. After connection, Agenvik shows the phone numbers available in that account.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/whatsapp-integration.png" alt="WhatsApp integration placeholder" />
</Frame>

After the workspace is connected, assign a phone number from the Agent's **WhatsApp Channel** card.

## Coming soon integrations

Slack, Discord, and Instagram appear as coming soon cards. They are visible so you can see the planned direction, but they are not configurable yet.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/coming-soon-integrations.png" alt="Coming soon integrations placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Disconnect carefully

Disconnecting a workspace integration can affect every Agent using it. For WhatsApp, disconnecting the workspace connection disables WhatsApp channels across Agents.
