USE CASE 1: SaaS Customer Support
-----------------------------------
Company type: A B2B SaaS product with a help center and documentation

Problem:
  Support team gets 200+ tickets per week. 70% are the same 15 questions:
  "How do I reset my password?", "Where is the billing page?",
  "How do I invite a teammate?", "What is the API rate limit?"
  Engineers and founders are pulled into support instead of building.

How Agenvik solves it:
  - Upload the help center docs as PDFs
  - Add common FAQ as Q&A pairs
  - Train the agent
  - Embed the web widget on the help center and dashboard
  - Enable Slack bot for internal team questions

Result:
  - 70% of tickets deflected automatically
  - Instant answers 24/7
  - Support team focuses on complex issues only
  - Customers get answers in seconds, not hours

Specific Agenvik features used:
  - PDF knowledge source (help center docs)
  - Q&A knowledge source (common tickets)
  - Web channel (embedded on app and help center)
  - Slack channel (internal team knowledge bot)
  - Analytics to identify remaining gaps in documentation

USE CASE 3: Agency Building Chatbots for Clients
--------------------------------------------------
Company type: A digital marketing or web development agency

Problem:
  - Clients want AI chatbots but can't build them
  - Building custom RAG systems for each client takes months
  - Clients have different knowledge bases, different branding, different channels
  - Ongoing maintenance is a nightmare

How Agenvik solves it:
  - Agency creates a separate agent per client
  - Each client uploads their own content
  - Agency configures branding (colors, name, avatar) per client
  - Embed code is given to the client to paste on their site

Result:
  - Agency can onboard a new client chatbot in 1 day
  - Recurring revenue from Agenvik subscription resold to clients
  - No engineering required — pure configuration
  - Clients can update their own knowledge base anytime

Specific Agenvik features used:
  - Multiple agents (one per client)
  - Custom web channel branding per agent
  - URL crawling (crawl client's existing website)
  - Behavior customization (different tone per client)
  - Subscription: Growth or Pro for multiple agents

COMMON OBJECTIONS & ANSWERS
-----------------------------

Objection: "We already have a chatbot"
Answer: Most chatbots use scripted decision trees — they only answer questions you pre-programmed.
Agenvik uses real AI with RAG, so it can answer any question that's in your knowledge base,
even questions you never anticipated. It's smarter and easier to maintain.

Objection: "We use ChatGPT for support"
Answer: ChatGPT doesn't know your specific product, policies, or pricing.
Agenvik trains the AI on YOUR content only. It won't hallucinate or give generic answers —
it answers from exactly what you uploaded.

Objection: "We'll just build this ourselves"
Answer: Building a RAG chatbot with multi-channel support, a management dashboard, analytics,
lead capture, and training pipelines takes 3-6 months of engineering work.
With Agenvik, you're live in 10 minutes.

Objection: "What if the AI gets something wrong?"
Answer: The AI only answers from the content you provide. If it doesn't find an answer
in your knowledge base, it uses your configured fallback message (e.g., "I don't know —
please contact us at support@yourcompany.com"). You're always in control.

Objection: "It's too expensive"
Answer: Compare to the cost of 1 support hire ($3,000-5,000/month salary + benefits).
Agenvik deflects 60-80% of repetitive tickets at a fraction of that cost.
There's also a free plan to start with no risk.

Objection: "We only need a web widget, not WhatsApp/Slack"
Answer: Start with just web. Enable other channels when you need them — it's one toggle.
You're not paying for channels you don't use.
