## SECTION 4 — How It Works for Support Teams

### H2
Set up in an afternoon. Live by tomorrow.

### Steps

**Step 1 — Upload your support docs**
Drop in your help articles, FAQ document, return policy, product guide — whatever you already have. No formatting required. Agenvik reads it and indexes it automatically.

**Step 2 — Set your fallback**
Decide what happens when the agent doesn't know the answer. Usually: "I don't have that information. Please email support@yourcompany.com and we'll reply within X hours." You write the message, we send it.

**Step 3 — Deploy on the channels your customers use**
Embed the widget on your website in 2 minutes. Connect your WhatsApp Business number in 15 minutes. Both can run the same agent simultaneously.

**Step 4 — Check the logs weekly**
See the questions your agent couldn't answer — those are your knowledge gaps. Add content, retrain. The agent gets better every week.

## SECTION 6 — FAQ (Support-specific)

**Q: Will it replace my support team?**
A: No — and that's intentional. Agenvik handles questions that have a definitive answer in your content. Anything complex, emotional, or edge-case gets routed to your human team, who now have more time and capacity for those conversations.

**Q: What if a customer asks something the agent doesn't know?**
A: The agent uses your fallback message — something like "I'm not sure about that. Please email support@yourcompany.com and we'll get back to you within 2 hours." It never guesses.

**Q: Can customers tell it's AI?**
A: Only if you want them to. Most businesses name the agent (e.g. "Aria") and don't label it as AI. That said, we always recommend being transparent — customers generally don't mind AI assistance if it actually helps them.

**Q: What channels does it work on?**
A: Website chat widget, WhatsApp, and Slack today. Instagram and Messenger are on the roadmap.
