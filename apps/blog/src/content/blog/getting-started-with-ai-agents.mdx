Artificial intelligence agents are no longer reserved for large engineering teams with massive budgets. With platforms like Agenvik, anyone can build a smart, responsive AI agent trained on their own knowledge — in minutes, not months.

This guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to a live, deployable AI agent.

## What Is an AI Agent?

An AI agent is a system that can understand questions and respond intelligently based on a body of knowledge you define. Unlike a generic chatbot, an AI agent built with Agenvik is trained specifically on *your* data — your FAQs, product docs, support articles, or any text you provide.

When a customer asks "How do I reset my password?", your agent doesn't guess. It finds the answer in the knowledge you've given it and responds accurately, in your tone.

## Step 1: Create Your Agenvik Account

Head to [agenvik.com](https://agenvik.com) and sign up for a free account. Once you're in, you'll land on the dashboard where you manage all your agents and knowledge sources.

The free tier lets you create one agent with up to 50 documents — more than enough to get started.

## Step 2: Create a New Agent

Click **New Agent** and give it a name. This is internal — your customers won't see it. Then choose a description that helps you remember its purpose (e.g., "Customer Support — E-commerce").

At this stage you can also configure the agent's personality: set a greeting message, choose a response tone (professional, friendly, concise), and define what topics it should stay focused on.

## Step 3: Add Your Knowledge

This is where the magic happens. Click **Add Source** and paste in text, upload documents, or connect a URL. Agenvik supports:

- Plain text and Markdown files
- PDFs and Word documents
- FAQ pages (paste the URL and Agenvik scrapes it automatically)
- CSV files with Q&A pairs

The more relevant content you add, the more accurately your agent can answer. Start with your top 20 most-asked questions.

## Step 4: Train and Test

Once your knowledge is uploaded, Agenvik indexes it automatically — this usually takes under a minute. Then open the **Test** panel and ask your agent a few questions.

Try to break it. Ask things your customers would ask, including edge cases. If an answer is wrong or incomplete, add better source material and re-test.

> **Tip:** The quality of your agent's answers is directly tied to the quality of your knowledge base. Clear, specific documentation produces better responses than vague or contradictory content.

## Step 5: Deploy

When you're happy with how the agent responds, click **Deploy**. You'll get:

- An **embeddable widget** you can drop onto any website with a single line of code
- A **WhatsApp integration** for customer conversations on mobile
- A **Slack integration** for internal team use

Copy the embed snippet, paste it into your website's HTML, and your agent is live — handling queries around the clock without any ongoing effort from you.

## What Comes Next?

After your first agent is live, explore Agenvik's analytics dashboard. You can see which questions are asked most, where your agent struggled, and which sources were referenced most often. Use this data to improve your knowledge base over time.

Building a great AI agent is an iterative process. Start simple, deploy fast, and refine based on real usage. That's the Agenvik way.
