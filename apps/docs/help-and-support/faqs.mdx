## Agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an Agent?">
    An Agent is an AI assistant configured for a specific use case. It has its own Knowledge Sources, Behaviour, Channels, Actions, and analytics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I not create another Agent?">
    Your workspace may have reached its active Agent limit. Archive an unused Agent or review your subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I restore an archived Agent?">
    Yes, if your workspace has active Agent capacity available for your plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Knowledge and training

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which Knowledge Sources are supported?">
    Agenvik currently supports Text sources and Q&A sources.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Train disabled?">
    Training can be disabled when no source is ready, the Agent is locked, or the daily training limit has been reached.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I use Text or Q&A?">
    Use Text for broader content and Q&A for precise answers to common or sensitive questions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Channels and integrations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I customize the web chat?">
    Open an Agent and edit the **Channel** card to set display name, subtitle, welcome message, input placeholder, and primary color.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is WhatsApp locked?">
    WhatsApp is available on paid plans. You also need to connect WhatsApp Business from **Integrations** before assigning a phone number to an Agent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can one WhatsApp number be used by multiple Agents?">
    No. A phone number can be assigned to one Agent at a time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Leads and analytics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I not see leads?">
    Lead Generation must be available on your plan, enabled on the Agent, and triggered during conversation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are Analytics charts locked?">
    Detailed Analytics are available on Starter and above. Overview cards may still be visible on other plans.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I improve an Agent after launch?">
    Review **Conversations** for weak answers and **Analytics** for patterns. Then update Knowledge Sources or Behaviour.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
