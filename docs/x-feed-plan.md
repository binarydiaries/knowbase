## Growth Tactics

- Reply to posts from @OpenAI, @LangChainAI, @vercel, @ProductHunt within the first hour of them posting
- Use hashtags sparingly: `#AI`, `#NoCode`, `#CustomerSupport` — max 2 per post
- Post between **9–11am IST** on weekdays for best reach
- Repost your best performing tweet every 3–4 weeks with a small edit
