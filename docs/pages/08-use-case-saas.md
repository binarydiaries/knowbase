## SECTION 2 — The Problem

### H2
The questions eating your CS team's time are answered in your docs. Your users just can't find them.

### Body
Your help center exists. Your docs are up to date. But users still open tickets because searching through docs is friction — and asking a human feels faster.

Meanwhile, your CS team answers "How do I connect X to Y?" for the hundredth time this week. And your activation rate is lower than it should be because new users get stuck and churn before they hit their first "aha" moment.

### The pattern:
1. User gets stuck on an integration or a feature
2. They either churn silently, or open a ticket
3. CS answers it with a link to the doc that was there all along
4. Repeat

## SECTION 6 — FAQ (SaaS specific)

**Q: Can the agent access real-time data like usage stats or subscription status?**
A: Not yet — Agenvik answers from static knowledge sources (documents, text). Dynamic data integration via API is on the roadmap. For queries like "what plan am I on?" route to your in-app account page using the fallback message.

**Q: Can I embed it inside my actual app, not just on the marketing site?**
A: Yes. The embed snippet works on any web page, including your authenticated app. Many teams run one agent on the marketing site and a separate, more technical agent inside the app.

**Q: What if users ask about a feature that was recently updated?**
A: Upload the updated docs and retrain. Changes go live in minutes. Most teams do a quick knowledge-base update whenever they ship a new feature.

**Q: Can I see which feature areas generate the most support questions?**
A: Yes — the conversation log and top questions panel show exactly which topics and queries come up most. Use this to prioritise documentation improvements and product changes.
