Today, we're officially launching **Agenvik**.

We built it because we kept running into the same problem: businesses of every size know they need AI agents, but building one that actually knows *your* product, speaks *your* brand's language, and works across *your* channels is still surprisingly hard — unless you have a dedicated engineering team.

Agenvik changes that.

## The Problem We're Solving

When a customer messages your support team at 2 AM asking how to reset their password, or a new user needs help figuring out your product's pricing during a holiday, there's no one there. You either staff 24/7 (expensive) or customers wait (frustrating).

Generic chatbots exist, but they're generic. They don't know your refund policy. They can't explain your product's specific features. They hallucinate. Customers see through them immediately, and trust erodes.

The alternative — building a custom AI agent — has traditionally required months of engineering work, ML expertise, and ongoing maintenance. That's out of reach for most teams.

We believe every business deserves an AI agent that actually knows what it's talking about. That's the gap Agenvik fills.

## What Agenvik Does

Agenvik lets you turn your existing knowledge into a working AI agent in minutes.

**You bring the knowledge. We handle everything else.**

Upload your FAQ documents, paste in your support articles, connect your help center URL — Agenvik indexes it all and makes it queryable by a conversational AI agent that responds accurately, in your tone, to your customers.

There's no prompt engineering. No fine-tuning. No infrastructure to manage. You describe what your agent should do, add your knowledge, and deploy.

## Where Your Agent Lives

One of the things we're most proud of is that you build once and deploy everywhere:

**Web widget** — A single line of code embeds your agent on any website. It loads fast, works on mobile, and matches your brand.

**WhatsApp** — Connect your business number and your agent handles customer queries directly in WhatsApp, where your customers already are.

**Slack** — Deploy internally for your team. Use it as a company knowledge base, an onboarding assistant, or an internal helpdesk.

Your customers and team interact through the channels they prefer. Your agent is consistent across all of them.

## How It Works

The underlying architecture is a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline — your documents are chunked, embedded, and stored as vectors. When a user asks a question, the most relevant passages are retrieved and fed to the language model alongside the query, so the answer is grounded in your actual content, not invented.

We've tuned this pipeline specifically for support and knowledge-base use cases. That means short, accurate answers rather than verbose AI prose — and citations to source documents when it matters.
