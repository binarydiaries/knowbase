## LinkedIn Notes

- Post 2–3 times per week maximum — LinkedIn penalises over-posting
- Always end with a question to drive comments
- First 2 lines are critical — they show before "see more" — make them impossible to ignore
- Founder posts outperform brand page posts — post from your personal account and share to the Agenvik page
- Avoid external links in the post body — put the link in the first comment instead to avoid LinkedIn reducing reach
