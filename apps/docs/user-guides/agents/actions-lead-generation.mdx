description: "Enable Lead Generation so your Agent can capture contact information through conversation."
---

**Actions** let an Agent do more than answer questions. The first available action is **Lead Generation**.

## What you will learn

- What Lead Generation captures
- How to enable and configure the action
- Where captured leads appear
- How plan access affects the feature

## Check access

Lead Generation is available on Starter and above. On the free plan, the action is locked.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/actions-card.png" alt="Actions card placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Enable Lead Generation

Open an Agent and find **Actions**.

1. Turn on **Lead Generation**.
2. Click **Configure** when the action is enabled.
3. Save the collection settings.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/lead-generation-config.png" alt="Lead Generation configuration placeholder" />
</Frame>

Lead Generation can capture name, email, phone, and company information through natural conversation.

## Test the flow

Ask the Agent a question that indicates buying intent or follow-up intent. Confirm that it asks for the right contact information and continues naturally.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/lead-generation-test.png" alt="Lead Generation test placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Review captured leads

Captured leads appear in **Leads**. You can filter by Agent and open the linked conversation when you need context.
