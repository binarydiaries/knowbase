## SECTION 2 — The Origin Story

Where this started

### Body
[PLACEHOLDER: Write the real founding story in 3–4 paragraphs. Below is a structure template — fill it in with real details.]

**Paragraph 1 — The observation:**
When we were [working at / building / talking to] [context], we kept seeing the same problem: businesses of every size had knowledge — detailed, accurate, hard-won knowledge about their products, policies, and processes — locked in PDFs, Google Docs, and help articles that customers could never actually find.

**Paragraph 2 — The failed alternatives:**
The existing solutions were either too expensive (enterprise AI deployments costing hundreds of thousands of dollars), too limited (rule-based chatbots that broke the moment someone asked anything off-script), or too generic (ChatGPT-style tools that answered from general knowledge, not the business's actual content).

**Paragraph 3 — The decision to build:**
We believed the technology to fix this was available — vector databases, retrieval-augmented generation, large language models — but nobody had packaged it for the businesses that needed it most: the e-commerce store with a 3-person team, the SaaS startup with 2 CS agents handling 500 tickets a week, the coaching business drowning in the same questions from every new cohort.

**Paragraph 4 — What Agenvik is:**
So we built Agenvik. A platform where anyone — without a data science team, without a prompt engineering background, without weeks of setup — can turn their existing knowledge into a working AI agent in under an hour. We're proud of that constraint. Simplicity is a design decision, not a shortcut.

## SECTION 5 — What We're Building Toward

### H2
Our point of view

### Body
[PLACEHOLDER: A short manifesto or mission statement. 2–3 paragraphs. Suggested themes below — write in the actual founders' voice.]

**Theme 1 — Access:**
The best AI tools today are built for enterprises with ML teams. We think the businesses that need AI most are the ones least equipped to build it themselves — the solo founder, the bootstrapped startup, the regional business scaling fast without the headcount. We're building for them.

**Theme 2 — Accuracy matters more than capability:**
Most AI agents hallucinate. They sound confident while being wrong. We built Agenvik specifically to be grounded in your content — to say "I don't know" when the answer isn't in the knowledge base, rather than making something up. We think trust is earned by getting it right, not by having the flashiest demo.

**Theme 3 — Simplicity as a moat:**
Every feature we build gets tested against one question: can a non-technical business owner use this without reading a tutorial? If not, we redesign it. Simplicity isn't a limitation — it's the product.
