POSITIONING STATEMENT
----------------------
For businesses that answer the same support questions over and over,
Agenvik is a no-code AI chatbot builder that trains on your own content
and deploys across your website, WhatsApp, and Slack in under 10 minutes.

Unlike generic AI tools or scripted chatbots,
Agenvik gives you a grounded AI that only answers from your knowledge base —
accurate, on-brand, and always available.


CATEGORY
---------
AI Customer Support Automation / Knowledge Base Chatbot Builder / RAG-as-a-Service


TARGET AUDIENCE
----------------
Primary: SaaS founders and customer success leads at companies with 5-100 employees
Secondary: E-commerce store owners, digital agencies, HR managers at mid-size companies
Jobs to be done:
  - Reduce support ticket volume without hiring more agents
  - Be available 24/7 for customer questions
  - Qualify and capture leads on the website
  - Build a multi-channel presence without engineering effort


COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
----------------------

VS. INTERCOM / ZENDESK AI
  Intercom and Zendesk are full customer support platforms (ticketing, CRM, live chat).
  Their AI features are add-ons to expensive platforms starting at $100-300+/month.
  They require a lot of setup and are overkill for small teams.
  Agenvik: purpose-built for AI-first support. Simpler, cheaper, faster to set up.
  Agenvik wins on: price, simplicity, setup time, multi-channel from day one.

VS. TIDIO / CRISP / FRESHCHAT
  These are live chat tools with basic chatbot builders.
  Their "AI" is mostly rule-based or keyword-matching — not true RAG.
  They don't train on your specific knowledge base deeply.
  Agenvik: real RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) with OpenAI embeddings.
  Agenvik wins on: answer quality, knowledge base depth, multi-channel.

VS. CHATBASE / CUSTOMGPT / SITEGPT
  These are the closest direct competitors — RAG chatbot builders.
  Most of them only deploy to web. WhatsApp/Slack integrations are limited or paid extras.
  Some lack analytics, lead capture, or behavior customization.
  Agenvik wins on: multi-channel (web + WhatsApp + Slack in one), richer analytics, lead capture.

VS. BUILDING IN-HOUSE
  Companies with engineering resources might build their own RAG chatbot.
  Reality: building + maintaining embeddings, chunking, retrieval, streaming, multi-channel,
  analytics, billing, and a management dashboard takes 3-6 months of eng time.
  Agenvik is ready in 10 minutes and maintained for you.
  Agenvik wins on: time to market, maintenance burden, total cost.

VS. CHATGPT / CLAUDE DIRECTLY
  People sometimes use AI chat tools manually for support. This doesn't scale.
  These tools have no knowledge of your specific product.
  Agenvik wins on: trained on your content, deployable, automated, branded.


KEY DIFFERENTIATORS (ranked by importance)
--------------------------------------------
1. Multi-channel from one agent
   Train once, deploy to web + WhatsApp + Slack. Most competitors do web only.

2. Grounded AI (no hallucination)
   Answers only from what you uploaded. Falls back gracefully when it doesn't know.

3. No-code, 10-minute setup
   Non-technical founders can set up a production-ready agent without any engineering.

4. Real-time streaming responses
   Feels fast and modern. Responses stream token-by-token, not after a 5-second wait.

5. Rich analytics on paid plans
   Sentiment, topics, confidence scores, country reach — tells you what your customers need.

6. Lead capture built in
   Convert support conversations into sales leads without a separate tool.

7. Transparent confidence scoring
   Every AI response includes a confidence score, so you know when to add more knowledge.
