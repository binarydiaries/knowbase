The **Account** page opens your profile and account management controls.

## What you will learn

- Where to manage profile details
- Where to update account security settings
- How account settings differ from workspace settings

## Open Account

Open the user menu, then choose **Account**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/account-menu.png" alt="Account page placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Manage profile details

Use Account to review profile information, email addresses, and authentication settings available to your user.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/account-profile.png" alt="Account profile placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Account versus workspace settings

Account settings apply to you as a signed-in user. Workspace-level features, such as Integrations, Agents, Leads, Usage, and subscription limits, apply to the organization or workspace.
