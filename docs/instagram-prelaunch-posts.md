## Instagram Notes

- Set link in bio to `agenvik.com` before posting
- Post the carousel at 6–9pm IST — Instagram engagement peaks in the evening
- Reply to every comment in the first 30 minutes after posting
- Use 5–7 hashtags maximum — more looks spammy
- Stories: share the carousel to stories on the same day with a "swipe up" or link sticker pointing to agenvik.com
- After launch, post 3–4 times per week: mix of reels, carousels, and single images
