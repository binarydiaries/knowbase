## How Agenvik is organized

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agents">
    Agents are the assistants you create for different teams, products, or customer journeys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Knowledge Sources">
    Knowledge Sources contain the text and Q&A content your agent uses when answering questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Behaviour">
    Behaviour controls instructions, tone, verbosity, fallback message, and guardrails.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Channels and Actions">
    Channels decide where the agent appears. Actions let the agent collect useful information, such as leads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inbox and Analytics">
    Conversations, Leads, Analytics, and Usage help you monitor outcomes after launch.
  </Step>
</Steps>
