Use this guide to isolate common issues before contacting support.

## What you will learn

- How to troubleshoot Agent setup
- How to handle disabled buttons and locked states
- How to check training, Channels, leads, and analytics

## Agent creation is disabled

Check whether your workspace has reached its active Agent limit. Archive an unused Agent or review your subscription.

## Knowledge Sources cannot be added

Check whether:

- The Agent is locked
- Your knowledge source limit has been reached
- Your subscription is over a workspace limit

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/knowledge-source-usage.png" alt="Knowledge Source limit troubleshooting placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Training does not start

Check whether:

- At least one source is ready
- Daily training runs remain
- The Agent is not locked
- The source content was saved before training

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/train-agent-dialog.png" alt="Training troubleshooting placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Agent gives weak answers

Review the answer against your Knowledge Sources. If the information is missing or vague, add a better Text source or Q&A source. If the answer has the wrong tone, update **Behaviour**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/behaviour-settings.png" alt="Weak answer troubleshooting placeholder" />
</Frame>

## WhatsApp cannot be assigned

Check whether:

- Your workspace is on a paid plan
- WhatsApp Business is connected in **Integrations**
- The phone number is not assigned to another Agent
- The Agent is not locked

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product-ss/whatsapp-channel.png" alt="WhatsApp assignment troubleshooting placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Leads are missing

Check whether:

- Lead Generation is enabled and configured
- Your plan includes Lead Generation
- The visitor provided contact details
- You are filtering the Leads table by the correct Agent

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/placeholders/leads-table.png" alt="Leads troubleshooting placeholder" />
</Frame>

## Analytics charts are unavailable

Detailed Analytics are available on Starter and above. If charts are locked, review your plan from the dashboard.
