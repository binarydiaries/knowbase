# Community code of conduct

Agenvik welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and community spaces is expected to:

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Give clear, constructive feedback and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the time of other contributors.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for people who are learning.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report community concerns privately through the contact listed on [Agenvik's support page](https://agenvik.com/docs/help-and-support/contact-support). Include links or context that help maintainers understand the situation. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for software vulnerabilities.

Maintainers will consider reports in context and may request changes, remove content, limit participation, or ban participants when needed. Reports should be handled with discretion, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable. These expectations also apply to maintainers.
