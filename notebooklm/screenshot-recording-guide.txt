SECTION D — TECHNICAL RECORDING SETTINGS
=============================================================

Recommended tool: Loom (loom.com) — free, easy, auto-uploads
Alternative: OBS Studio (free, local recording, more control)
Mac built-in: Shift+Cmd+5 → Record Selected Portion

Settings:
  - Resolution: 1920x1080
  - Frame rate: 30fps minimum (60fps preferred)
  - Audio: Record your voice with a headset mic (not laptop mic — too much echo)
  - Cursor: Make cursor visible and slightly larger (Accessibility settings on Mac/Windows)
  - Cursor highlight: Use Loom's cursor spotlight or ScreenBrush for Mac

Before each clip:
  - Navigate to the right URL
  - Pause 2 seconds before doing anything (gives editing room)
  - Speak your narration line clearly BEFORE starting the action
  - Pause 2 seconds at the end (gives editing room)

Mouse movement tips:
  - Move slowly — fast mouse movements are hard to follow on video
  - Don't hover randomly — hover intentionally on elements you're talking about
  - When clicking something important, pause briefly AFTER the click to let the result show
