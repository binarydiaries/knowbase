### Feature: Website Chat Widget
**Headline:** Embed on any website with one line of code
**Body:** Copy the snippet from your dashboard. Paste it before `</body>` on any webpage. The chat widget appears in the bottom-right corner — clean, unobtrusive, branded to your agent. Works on Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, Squarespace, or any custom site. No frameworks, no build step.

**What you can customise today:**
- Agent name and avatar shown in the widget header
- Greeting message ("Hi there! How can I help?")
- Widget position (bottom-left or bottom-right)

**Coming soon:**
- Widget colour theming to match your brand
- Mobile-specific layout options

## SECTION 6 — Customisation

### Eyebrow
✦ CUSTOMISATION

### H2

---

### Feature: Tone & Personality
**Headline:** Formal or friendly — you decide
**Body:** Choose from preset tone profiles or write your own behaviour instructions. A legal firm's agent sounds different from a streetwear brand's agent. Both can use the same platform.

**Tone options (presets):**
- **Formal** — Professional language, full sentences, no contractions
- **Friendly** — Warm and approachable, uses first names, conversational
- **Direct** — Short answers, no filler, gets to the point immediately
- **Supportive** — Empathetic, patient, suited to customer service

**Custom instructions:** Write specific instructions like "Always end responses with a suggested next step" or "Never mention competitor products."

[ILLUSTRATION: Behaviour settings panel. A tone slider with the 4 presets. A "Custom instructions" text area with the example instruction typed in.]

### Feature: Fallback Behaviour
**Headline:** Control what happens when your agent doesn't know
**Body:** When a question isn't answered by the knowledge base, your agent won't guess — it will follow your fallback instruction. Set it to say "I don't have that information. Please email [email]" or route to a specific contact method. You write the message.
[ILLUSTRATION: Fallback settings: a text field with the default message "I'm not sure about that. For more help, contact support@yourcompany.com — we reply within 2 hours."]
